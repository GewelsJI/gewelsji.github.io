# Ge-Peng Ji (季葛鹏)

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Mohamed bin Zayed University of Artificial Intelligence (MBZUAI) in Abu Dhabi. I earned my PhD at the Australian National University and my master’s degree at Wuhan University.

[Academic homepage](https://gewelsji.github.io/) · [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=oaxKYKUAAAAJ&hl=en) · [Blog & Resources](https://gewelsji.github.io/resources/)

## Research

I study **subtle visual perception (微视觉感知)**, focusing on visual patterns that are easy to miss but carry important information. My work spans healthcare AI, camouflaged scene understanding, and high-precision vision.
